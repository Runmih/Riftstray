## Controls

- Chapter selection offers direct default entry or `Configure Mihata (Prototype)`. Increase prototype level to grant exactly one skill point and four attribute points per gained level; purchase eligible Sword passives or allocate attributes, then start the configured mission. Reset Build refunds allocations/skills but does not restart a running mission.
- Keyboard: arrows move the visible grid cursor; Enter selects Mihata, commits a highlighted destination, and opens the nearby Attack/Wait/Back menu. Attack focuses a legal target; Enter opens the forecast and a second, released press explicitly confirms. Mouse follows the same unit → destination/actions → target → forecast path. Escape steps back one level; from browsing it opens Pause, during playback it pauses, and only Pause → Return to Chapters exits. Committed movement is never refunded. Attack/Wait ends Mihata's activation; End Turn forfeits it. Show calculations expands the bounded damage-type, mitigation, matchup, block-overflow, weight/Attack Speed, follow-up, and critical breakdown.
- Courtesans (`C1`–`C6`) and enemies (`E1`–`E3`) act sequentially after the player. Green tiles are exits; ROCK tiles block movement.
- Restart Mission resets only mission state. Pause → Return to Chapters is the explicit battle exit; pending AI or strike presentation is cancelled and all cosmetic transforms are restored.

Iron Sword/Axe/Spear critical values are 5%/10%/5% and weights are 3/5/4; Iron Shield weight is 2. The mission remains physical-only.

## Manual playtest recipe

1. Without the mouse: Main Menu → Select World → Red Alchemist → Chapter One. Use arrows and Enter to select Mihata, move once, open nearby actions, choose Attack, select a target, cancel the forecast, reopen it, and explicitly Confirm Attack.
2. Read damage/hit/crit, HP and white potential-loss segments at a glance. Expand calculations once, then watch each strike for lunge plus hit/dodge/block/break/Riposte/death feedback without relying on the log.
3. During a strike press Escape to pause, then Escape to resume. From normal browsing open Pause and use its explicit Restart/Return choices. Confirm movement is not refunded and no delayed visual remains after restart/return.
4. Open Playback Settings (Dev), try 0.25, 1.00, and 3.00 seconds per strike, and report the most readable value. Confirm unit tokens remain recognizable: blue player, green courtesans, red/purple enemies (yellow is reserved for friendly combatants).
